The three Walls and their districts: four ways to draw them

Each card shows the whole wall system on the island's real coastline (left) and the south close up, from Ehrmich to Shiganshina (right). A is the map as it is now. In B, C and D the inner Walls are drawn smaller, as in your two pictures, which leaves room for districts that stick out further than a half circle.

# 3D atlas art direction

Keep the painted atlas alive: yellow-green fields, deep moss woods, warm limestone, terracotta roofs and muted blue water. Rounded flesh contrasts with faceted buildings and terrain. Parchment sunlight and cool slate night connect to the page; ember red belongs to danger. Silhouette and motion carry character before facial detail.

**Titan silhouettes.** titan1 is the 15 m narrow, dark-haired giant: long legs, slack arms, an absent-minded head turn. titan2 is the 10 m pot belly: short legs, deep abdomen and a broad waddle. titan3 is the 12 m hunch: head thrust forward, dark hair and hands near the knees; its chest lurches before the next step. titan4 is the 6 m baby face: huge round bald head, small legs and unsettled toddler sway. titan5 is the 13 m gaunt giant: narrow trunk, long face, projecting jaw and stiff, distracted arms. titan6 is the 8 m abnormal in a low four-point stance: horizontal back, forward head and rapid scuttle. Ragako lies helplessly on its back, face upward, with wasted limbs; only breath moves. The crawler is belly-down, pulling alternately with its arms while both legs trail. Never exchange those poses. Wall Titans are 60 m red muscle columns with pale jaw and tendon accents: nearly motionless at attention, then upright and relentless with minimal arm swing.

**People and places.** Preserve brown, dusty blue and rust civilian clothes; the woman's skirt and smaller child must read at a glance. Panic raises civilian arms; soldiers keep purposeful swings, short tan jackets, white trousers and brown boots. The chestnut horse carries a green-cloaked rider. Houses have steep gables; Mitras has a central spire and two towers, Reiss a slate chapel roof, Utgard an uneven broken tower. Barges carry five seated colour-and-head pairs. The train is soot black with brass and oxblood wagons. Unspecified building dimensions are plausible art dimensions, not surveyed canon.

**Walls and gates.** Use 50 m limestone faces, six broad courses, a pale top and shallow dark joints. The parapet adds 1.5 m above the main wall. Gates are dark arches, 12 m wide and 16 m high including the arch, with pale frames and restrained gold relief. Keep inner and outer district gates separate. Breach amber differs from lost-territory orange. Broken gates have dark voids, scattered stone and a brief ember cue; crystal is pale mint, while Trost's plug is opaque earthy stone. A broken outer gate does not erase the whole Wall.

**Do not get these wrong.** Geometry and animation distances are metres; map units are 2,000 m. Heights describe standing bodies even for prone or seated poses; scene exaggeration is separate. Children retain their smaller scale. Child joints inherit joint transforms, never a primitive's centre or size. Negative shoulder rx reaches forward. Walk phase follows distance; one stride means two steps, with left and right legs half a cycle apart. Sine gaits approximate planted feet. Align the rider's seat pivot to the animated saddle. Conceal the bottom 10 m of embedded Wall Titans and reveal them through story state. Preserve night inactivity, protected towns, distinct gate states and destruction timing. Fog and steam must leave routes and rings readable. Share primitive instances and use emissive windows. Transform, mounting, waterline and effect conventions are in camera.notes.

## Capture, bites and broken masonry

**Sequence.** Blend idle into the held `grab` pose over 0.45 s, close the grip and pause for 0.15 s; lift by blending `grab` into `eat` over 0.85 s. Run one `eat` cycle (1.2–1.6 s), then release/drop over 0.3 s and recover to idle over 0.5 s. For titan2, lengthen the crouch to 0.65 s and lift to 1.1 s; for titan5, shorten them to 0.3 s and 0.65 s. These transition times are separate from clip periods: `grab` is a sustained pose with small breathing motion, not a repeated snatch. Keep feet seated during blends. Attach the caught person's torso centre to the evaluated right forearm end, not its ground-space origin, and run `held` continuously during grip and lift (0.44–0.56 s cycles). Preserve the person's existing scale. Release from the current attachment with a short downward fall and fade the intact silhouette; no detached parts.

**Character and contact.** titan1 folds its long legs into a deliberate, slack-backed scoop; titan2 settles heavily behind its belly and chews slowly; titan3 reaches furthest with its long arms and heaves through the chest; titan4 spreads its balancing arm and rocks through quick bites; titan5 keeps an off-centre stare and gives sharp triple nods. All use angular joints without stretching limbs. The eating head nods 8–14 degrees at harmonic 2 or 3. titan6 keeps four limb ends planted: blend into its head-first `grab` over 0.25 s, attach at the mouth, lift the head into `eat` over 0.4 s, chew/shake for 1.25 s, then release over 0.2 s. Its body stays low; only the head shakes during chewing.

**Blood.** Trigger one small burst at each forward bite peak of the head rx channel, not once per frame: 12 muted brick-red drops, a sparse warm-red mist and a 0.65 s lifetime. Emit forward and slightly downward at the animated mouth; speed is m/s, gravity is downward m/s², and sizes are diameters in metres. Darken falling drops toward `dark`; use one 2.4 m oxblood stain beneath the bite position, fading over 24 s. The mouth spray and ground mark should read at map scale without anatomical detail or a persistent red cloud.

**Breach.** Tear a ragged 26 × 29 m opening around the existing 12 × 16 m gate. Use fresh pale fracture faces against a dark soot rim, 32 irregular blocks scattered up to 24 m on both sides, and a continuing, translucent smoke column rising 42 m above the breach base. The opening must cut the masonry silhouette and reveal the space behind it. Damage stays local to the affected gate; preserve the rest of the Wall and the distinction between inner and outer gates.

**Ruin.** During the Rumbling, break each released stretch into 2–6 m falling chunks over 3.6 s, with 24 chunks per 100 m. Leave irregular 1.5–6 m stumps separated by open gaps and broad, three-tone limestone rubble heaps; avoid a uniformly shortened intact ring. Warm dust at 0.34 opacity briefly traces the collapse without hiding routes or departing Titans. Apply to all three Walls and their district arcs at the existing story destruction times.
